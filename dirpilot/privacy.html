Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Summary

DirPilot is a Windows desktop tool that helps you inspect and carry out local folder transfer workflows. DirPilot processes the folders and files you select on your device. The developer does not receive your files or folder information through the app.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • DirPilot does not upload your files, file names, folder paths, or transfer results.
  • No in-app telemetry, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No automatic crash reporting operated by DirPilot.

Information DirPilot Accesses on Your Device

When you choose a Source folder and a Target folder, DirPilot accesses those locations at your direction to provide its core functionality. This local access may include:

  • Reading folder structure, file names, file contents, file-system metadata, and availability or access state in the selected Source.
  • Inspecting the selected Target and the existing items that may be affected by a transfer.
  • Creating directories and files in the selected Target and overwriting files that have the same relative path when you start a push.
  • Rechecking Source information after a push to determine whether the Source changed while the operation was running.

This processing occurs locally on your device. DirPilot does not send this information to the developer or to a DirPilot-operated service. Files that exist only in Target are kept by the current release.

Information We Collect or Receive

DirPilot does not collect, receive, transmit, or store on the developer's servers your files, file contents, file names, folder paths, transfer plans, transfer results, or diagnostic logs. DirPilot does not include an analytics SDK, advertising SDK, user account system, cloud synchronization service, or developer-operated crash reporter.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer will receive the information you choose to include in that message. Do not send private files, credentials, or sensitive folder contents unless they are needed for your request and you understand the risks of doing so.

Support Communications

If you contact the developer, your email address, message, and any information you choose to provide are used to respond to your request, investigate issues, provide support, protect the Software and its users, and meet legal obligations. Support messages are stored through the developer's email service provider and are protected using the reasonable account and service safeguards available for that provider.

The developer does not sell support communications or use them for advertising. They may be disclosed to service providers or professional advisers when reasonably necessary to handle your request, or when required by law, legal process, or the protection of rights, safety, and security.

Local Application Data

DirPilot stores only limited application data in the current Windows user's local application data area:

  • A versioned setting indicating whether the user has previously acknowledged the risk of overwriting matching Target files.
  • While a push may be in progress, a safety marker containing a schema version, a randomly generated run identifier, and a UTC start time. This marker allows DirPilot to warn the same Windows user after an interrupted operation.

These local records do not contain Source or Target paths, file names, file contents, transfer plans, transfer results, or error details. DirPilot does not save recent folders, saved tasks, or transfer history in the current release.

Retention and Deletion

Local application data remains on your device only as needed for the settings and interruption-safety behavior described above. Under the packaged Windows application lifecycle, this data is intended to remain available across normal updates and Repair, and to be removed by Windows when you Reset or uninstall DirPilot. Actual platform-level handling is controlled by Windows and the installation technology.

Support communications are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain an appropriate support record, resolve disputes, protect rights and security, and satisfy legal obligations. You may request deletion using the contact address below, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate security, legal, or compliance purposes.

DirPilot does not delete or clean up your selected Source or Target when the app is uninstalled. Those folders remain under your control.

Network Access and External Links

DirPilot does not require an account and does not contact a DirPilot-operated server. It does not perform its own update checks; installation and updates are handled through the Microsoft Store.

If you choose a legal-document link in DirPilot, Windows opens the exact public HTTPS page in your default browser. Your browser, GitHub Pages, network provider, and Microsoft may process ordinary connection data under their own terms and privacy notices. DirPilot does not receive the contents of that browser session.

Microsoft and Windows Services

Microsoft Store, Windows, WebView2, Windows Error Reporting, and related platform services may process installation, update, device, account, diagnostic, crash, or usage information under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. These services are operated by Microsoft, not by DirPilot, and their collection and retention are controlled by Microsoft and your Windows or Microsoft account settings.

The developer may access Microsoft Partner Center reports concerning acquisition, installation, usage, app health or crashes, ratings, and reviews. Such reports are used to distribute, support, diagnose, and improve DirPilot. DirPilot does not intentionally attach your Source or Target paths, file names, file contents, transfer plans, or transfer results to those reports, and it does not add its own telemetry or automatic crash-reporting channel.

Security and User Control

You control which existing Source and Target folders DirPilot accesses by selecting them through the application. DirPilot performs safety and access checks before enabling a push, and asks for confirmation before the first real overwrite operation. You can cancel folder selection, choose different folders, stop an active push at a file boundary, or uninstall the app.

No software can guarantee absolute security. Keep appropriate backups of important files and carefully review Source, Target, warnings, and the preflight summary before starting a push.

Children's Privacy

DirPilot is a developer and power-user utility and is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required by applicable law. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights relating to personal information. Because DirPilot does not send the local folder and file information described in this policy to the developer, the developer does not have that information to access, correct, export, or delete. For information you voluntarily send by email, you may contact the developer using the address below to request access, correction, or deletion. You may also manage Microsoft and Windows data through the privacy controls provided by Microsoft. These rights may be subject to exceptions under applicable law.

International Users

DirPilot's folder and file processing occurs locally and is not transmitted by the app to the developer. Accessing this public policy or contacting the developer uses third-party internet services and may involve processing in other countries under those providers' policies.
